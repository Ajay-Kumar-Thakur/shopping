Orders
How do I place an order?

Browse our shop, choose a product, fill in your details and click "Confirm Order". You will receive a confirmation email shortly after.

Can I cancel my order?

You can cancel your order within 24 hours of placing it. After that, please contact our support team and we will do our best to assist you.

Shipping
How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days within Nepal. Express delivery (1–2 days) is available at checkout for an additional fee.

Do you offer free shipping?

Yes! We offer free standard shipping on all orders over NPR 2,000 within Kathmandu Valley. Orders outside the valley may have a small delivery charge.

Returns
What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery for unused items in original packaging. Please contact us to initiate a return and we will arrange a pickup.
